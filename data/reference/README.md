# Reference data

Small, committed datasets the pipeline needs. **Every file states its source and retrieval date** in the table below. Nothing personal; nothing large.

| File | What | Source | Retrieved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cells-ottawa.csv` | Cell carriers within 10 km of Carleton that our radios can tune (600–1750 MHz): lat, lon, height, carrier, frequency, bandwidth, location, distance. Built by `analysis/extract_cells.py`. `height_m` is ground elevation plus antenna height as filed, but 1,328 of 1,971 rows (67 %) have no ground elevation and hold the antenna height alone; scenarios correct this with `tower_height_m` (below 80 m, TBC). The same site is sometimes filed twice a few metres apart; scenarios count it once. No PCI or EARFCN: the registry doesn't list them | [ISED Spectrum Licences Site Data](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-system/en/download-sms-data), `Site_Data_Extract.zip` (file dated 2024-10-30) | 2026-11-08 |
| `lte-cells-ottawa.csv` | One row per registered LTE sector within 10 km of Carleton that our radios can tune (600–1750 MHz, ≥ 1.4 MHz wide): EARFCN, PCI, band, downlink MHz, bandwidth, licensee, PLMN, TAC, ECI, eNB ID (ECI >> 8), local cell ID, lat, lon, antenna height above ground, azimuth, beamwidth, power, location, flags, dates, distance. Built by `analysis/extract_lte_cells.py`. EARFCNs are computed from the filed frequency (lowest band number where bands overlap, `TBC` against what the cells broadcast). `flags`: `tx_rx_swapped` (the filing's tx sits in an uplink range, so its rx is used), `off_raster`, `nonstandard_bw`. No ground elevation in this extract. Contact and account fields are never read | [ISED Terrestrial Spectrum Licence Site Data Extract](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-system/en/download-sms-data), `Site_Data_Extract_FX.zip` (file dated 2026-11-03, rows uploaded 2026-04-30 to 2026-11, SHA-256 `b9a81941b0d13d821d2cd4242876204d5410f41cc4da20ebfc0ed715b307c0f3`) | 2026-11-09 |
| `lte-scan-list-ottawa.csv` | The carriers to scan for PCIs (#87): one row per EARFCN and licensee, with how many registered sites and cells use it, how many sites lie within 3 km, the nearest site's distance, and the flags above. Sorted by sites within 3 km. Built by `analysis/extract_lte_cells.py` | Same as `lte-cells-ottawa.csv` | 2026-11-09 |
| `sionna-campus-route.csv` | Ray-traced line of sight, excess delay and gain from five TELUS sites along the 150 m loop, every 5 s, with each tower's lat/lon so the sim can refuse a mismatch. Built by `analysis/sionna_campus.py`; the first trace listed one Bank St mast twice, and that duplicate's rows were removed by hand. Assumed heights and materials, no trees, walls opaque (pessimistic at 886 MHz) (ADR-0016) | Sionna RT 2.2.0 over [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) buildings (ODbL). © OpenStreetMap contributors | 2026-11-08 |
| `navaids.csv` | The 19 VOR, VOR-DME, VORTAC, DME and NDB stations within 150 km (horizontal, WGS84) of Carleton, nearest first: ident, name, type, frequency (kHz; a DME's is its paired VHF frequency), lat, lon, elevation (ft above sea level, blank where unpublished), slaved variation (the variation a VOR's radials are aligned to), magnetic variation (degrees, east positive), DME channel, country, airport, OurAirports id, distance. Built by `analysis/extract_navaids.py`; values copied as published. Four VORs (YOW on 114.6 MHz, MSS, YMX, YUL), one DME and 14 NDBs. Crowd-maintained, not the official source: positions, frequencies and variations are `TBC` against the Canada Flight Supplement (NAV CANADA) before a real session relies on them. Mirabel (YMX) lists 1,663 ft, which looks wrong for an airport near 270 ft. The full file (1.5 MB, SHA-256 `d7292380662c33a95214945023ec0a5b13189599305fd18be739e167a97a9bd1`, last modified 2026-11-10) stays out of git | [OurAirports](https://davidmegginson.github.io/ourairports-data/navaids.csv) (David Megginson and contributors), `navaids.csv`. Public domain: "This is free and unencumbered software released into the public domain" ([Unlicense](https://github.com/davidmegginson/ourairports-data/blob/main/LICENSE)) | 2026-11-10 |

Kelso), from US Space Force 18 SDS data. Credit CelesTrak; usage terms `TBC` against celestrak.org | 2026-11-08 |
| `tle-gps-20261111.txt` | Orbital elements of the 32 operational GPS satellites (`gps-ops`), one three-line set each, the PRN in the name (e.g. `GPS BIIR-5  (PRN 22)`). Epochs 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-10. For the GNSS sky-map spoof check (`pnt.integrity.sky`, #206, ADR-0041): which PRNs are above the horizon at a place and time, and their elevation. Copied unchanged (CRLF line ends as served), SHA-256 `ea046237b093d910385855eb3fd82a52f4d7d55a034ed15c69c3f550ea8cd1b0`. Kelso), from US Space Force 18 SDS data. Credit CelesTrak; usage terms `TBC` against celestrak.org | 2026-11-11 (06:29:50 UTC) |
| `tle-starlink-20261110.txt` | Orbital elements of the 682 Starlink satellites (of 10,621 in the file) that rise above 10° over Carleton between 2026-11-12 18:00:00 and 18:15:00 UTC (the scenarios' start, `scenario.T0_NS`), checked every 5 s with SGP4; for the simulated Starlink layer (P13, #97). Built by `analysis/extract_starlink_tles.py`; each satellite's three lines are copied unchanged. Epochs 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-10, so propagated 2 to 4 days to the scenario time: Starlink satellites manoeuvre, so these are realistic orbits, not a prediction of where each satellite will be. The 10° cut leaves room below the scenarios' `TBC` 25° mask. The full file (1.8 MB, SHA-256 `27c8bb604df12fda7246a2c8cd563bbd61a4d3725afe8df4264c598e0ad8fad5`) stays out of git | [CelesTrak](https://celestrak.org/NORAD/elements/gp.php?GROUP=starlink&FORMAT=tle) (T.S. Kelso), from US Space Force 18 SDS data. Credit CelesTrak; usage terms `TBC` against celestrak.org | 2026-11-10 |

`cells-ottawa.csv`, `lte-cells-ottawa.csv` and `lte-scan-list-ottawa.csv` are extracts of *Spectrum Licences Site Data* by Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada, reproduced for non-commercial use under the [Government of Canada terms](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/terms-and-conditions). It is derived from the version at the source link above (filtered by distance, technology and frequency; heights summed in `cells-ottawa.csv`; EARFCN, eNB ID and distance computed); it is not an official version and is not endorsed by ISED. The SHA-256 of the 2024 source zip behind `cells-ottawa.csv` was not recorded; the 2026 zip's is in the table. Commercial redistribution needs ISED's written permission.

Until the remaining TODO files exist, scenarios use **synthetic** emitter geometry, labelled as such in their YAML.
