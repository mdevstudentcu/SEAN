# ADR-0036: The LTE error model is split into white and correlated parts, sized from real captures

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-10, amended 2026-11-11 (the front end's real-capture fixes, decided by the owners: see *Amendment 2026-11-11 (owners)*; the numbers regenerated at `8892fca` with those fixes)
- **Deciders:** team (the owners accept or change each point below)
- **Traces to:** REQ-01, REQ-02, REQ-10, ADR-0015, ADR-0022, ADR-0030, #147, #173, #183

## Context

Every LTE error size in the sim is `TBC`: 50 m white range noise at 1 Hz, 50 m Gauss-Markov multipath (30 s), tower clock drift 0.0354 m/s per builder and per site, a 0.4 ppm dongle, and records that report 2 m (`told_sigma_m`, `challenge-*`). #147 showed the filters do worse with LTE than without it when the reported sigma is optimistic.

On 2026-11-10 the payload recorded static LTE captures at two spots (one RTL-SDR, no GPS fix): 60 s per carrier, and 1,800 s on EARFCN 5060. `analysis/lte_measured_noise.py` measured what static captures without truth can show. Every number and its session are in `analysis/results/lte-measured-noise/summary.md`; how it was made and the commits: `NOTES.md` there (front end and analysis at `8892fca`, #245's merge, all 15 sessions; the values before #245, at `e9a9053`, are in the amendment). In short:

- **White noise of a 10 Hz record follows SNR:** robust σ 0.73 m at 13 to 16 dB, 2.4 m at −2 to 1 dB, 10.9 m at −11 to −8 dB, 22.5 m at −14 to −11 dB (43 records only). Fitted on the bins at SNR ≥ −8 dB only (`TBC`): σ² = 0.74² + 2.32² / SNR_lin (m), within 0.82 to 1.18 of each fitted bin (0.82 at −8 to −5 dB). Below −8 dB the law does not hold: the fit reads 6.5 m against the measured 10.9 m at −11 to −8 dB and 8.7 m against 22.5 m at −14 to −11 dB, and a fit over every bin (0.72² + 2.34² / SNR_lin) is no better. The front end's reported σ is 1.3 to 2.0 times *larger* than the measured white part.
- **A correlated part the reported σ cannot see:** inside one arc the error grows from the white σ at 0.1 s to S(1 s) of 2.4 to 17 m (median 8.1 m) on the 19 cells with SNR above 0 dB tracked 80 % or more, and to 5.5 to 36 m at 1 s (median 9.1 m; 8.2 to 25 m at 10 s) in 7 pairs of sectors of one eNB, which share a clock. The 36 m pair (`1623-…-e5145`, 347 − 175) has a cell on a 200 Hz carrier alias; without it 5.5 to 18 m. So it is not a clock: multipath changing at a static antenna, or the tracker's loop. Which one is `TBD`.
- **Tower clocks:** sectors of one eNB drift together within 0.5 m/s at two standard errors (−0.15 ± 0.16 m/s on the 1,800 s capture, 150 pieces, 1,281 s fitted). Of five 60 s pairs, three agree with zero within 2.1 standard errors (−0.60 ± 0.29, −1.25 ± 0.76 and −1.41 ± 1.08 m/s; the last read −16.3 m/s before #245, a front-end artefact) and two do not: +0.53 ± 0.15 m/s (`1623-…-e5060`, 492 − 4, 3.5 standard errors) and +3.22 ± 1.02 m/s (`1623-…-e5255`, 347 − 175, 3.2). No shared clock does that, so a front-end effect remains in some 60 s pairs (`TBD`). Pairs not shown to be one eNB (by the table `other eNB` or `TBD`): the 1,800 s capture gives −0.61 ± 0.24 and −0.07 ± 0.19 m/s (2.6 and 0.4 standard errors). In 60 s sessions with the weaker cell above −8 dB (21 pairs), up to 10.3 m/s of either sign; seven at 3.5 to 14.5 standard errors: +10.32 ± 0.71 (`lte-outside-5145`, 188 − 175, both cells above 13 dB), −7.80 ± 1.47 (`1623-…-e2460`, 244 − 220), −6.53 ± 0.66 (`1623-…-e5205`, 330 − 20), −3.23 ± 0.68, −2.37 ± 0.41 and +2.35 ± 0.43 (`1610-…-e5060`) and −2.38 ± 0.68 m/s (`1623-…-e2560`, 347 − 175). They need not be clocks: on `lte-outside-5145`, 189 − 175 should read (189 − 188) + (188 − 175) = +8.9 m/s and reads −2.06 ± 0.83. With the weaker cell below −8 dB, one pair: −23.7 ± 6.5 m/s (`1610-…-e5205`, 325 − 20, weaker cell −8.9 dB, 3.7 standard errors). The script applies no SNR cut to the drift table; −8 dB is the cut its pair structure figure uses (`snr_min_db > -8`), and short sessions' errors understate correlated noise. So the inter-eNB drift is `TBD`: up to about 24 m/s (79 ppb) seen, 10.3 m/s (34 ppb) with both cells above −8 dB, neither a value nor a bound the data defends. The sim's 0.05 m/s (pair) is inside both.
- **Clock Allan deviations are bounds only.** The loop-filtered CFO of a same-eNB pair reads 5e-9 to 2e-8 (1 to 50 s), so it cannot see the clocks. One cell's timing (dongle, tower, multipath change and tracker together) grows to S(100 s) = 141 m, σ_y 8.1e-9, against the sim dongle's 9.8e-10 at 100 s. Neither may replace `offset_rw_m` or `drift_rw_mps`.
- **Front-end faults** left after #183's fixes (#245): 4 of 40 cell tracks have a carrier offset within 0.1 of a whole multiple of 200 Hz (1 / 5 ms) from the strongest cell on the same dongle, and per-cell code-against-carrier rate disagrees by −146 to +222 ppb (8 of 39 cells beyond ±100 ppb; the `5205` ones near ±133 ppb, 100 Hz at 751 MHz). Doppler records are off by default (amendment, 5), so this reaches the CFO in each pseudorange's meta and the CFO Allan bound, not a record the filters use. The 2 weak cells (SNR below −8 dB) break 11 and 19 times a minute; cells at 0 dB or more a median 4 times a minute (0 to 14). On the 1,800 s capture the strongest cell starts a new arc every 10 s (181 arcs), the others 86 and 22 arcs, so the structure function inside one arc reaches 20 s on PCI 4 and 200 s on PCI 492. With the recorder's buffer stamps the front end flags 2 dropped-sample gaps that the recorder does not; the long-lag S(10 s) and S(100 s) agree with and without the stamps within 5 %.

A static capture cannot show bias (no position), the size of multipath (a static antenna's multipath is an offset the arc constant absorbs), how multipath changes with motion, or another site.

## Options

| Option | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Keep every placeholder | Nothing moves; results stay paired with `main` | 50 m white at 1 Hz is 3 to 10 times what was seen at rest; the 2 m told σ is what #147 showed breaks the filters |
| B. Put the static numbers straight into the sim | Measured, not assumed | A static antenna is the best case: it understates multipath on a moving drone. One afternoon, two spots in one neighbourhood |
| C. Split the model: the front end reports a white σ from SNR and, apart from it, a correlated term; the sim's white noise is set from the measurement and its correlated term is kept as an upper value `TBC` until a moving capture with GPS sizes it | Each part is sized by what can measure it; the filter is told the correlated part instead of a too-small white σ | A meta field and a filter change; sim results stop pairing with `main` |

## Decision

Option C, in the steps below, each proposed for the owners.

## Consequences

1. **Front-end reported σ (10 Hz record):** at SNR ≥ −8 dB (`TBC`), σ_white² = 0.74² + 2.32² / SNR_lin (m, `TBC`, fit above; the per-bin values and the fit are in `summary.json`); below −8 dB, never the fit but the measured bin value, 10.9 m at −11 to −8 dB and 22.5 m at −14 to −11 dB (`TBC`; the second on 43 records); below −14 dB nothing was measured (`TBD`). This replaces `sigma_scale` × the timing statistic. In addition `sigma_corr_m` (10 m, `TBC`: about the median, 9.1 m, of the 5.5 to 36 m same-eNB S(1 s)) and `tau_corr_s` (3 s, `TBC`: S(τ) levels off between 1 and 10 s) in each record's meta, for the filter to carry as a per-cell Gauss-Markov state, never to average down as white. A 1 Hz consumer is then told about √(σ_white² / 10 + σ_corr²), 10 m for a strong cell, against 2 m today. Needs a front-end test and a change to the ICD's meta list (`icd` label).
2. **Doppler records** from a cell whose carrier offset is more than 100 Hz (`TBC`) from the strongest cell's on the same dongle are flagged `carrier_alias` and dropped by the estimators until #183 is fixed.
3. **Sim (`challenge-*`, `realistic`):** `told_sigma_m` follows (1). `sigma_m` at 1 Hz becomes 10 m (`TBC`, bounded by the measured S(1 s), median 8.1 m over the 19 cells with SNR above 0 dB tracked 80 % or more), labelled `measured: 20261110 static sessions` with the bound stated. `multipath` stays 50 m / 30 s as an upper value `TBC` until the moving capture. Tower `drift_mps` stays `TBC`: the measurement bounds it between sectors (≤ 0.5 m/s), not between eNBs (`TBD`: up to 24 m/s seen, 10.3 m/s with both cells above −8 dB), and supports ADR-0022's shared site clock, but gives no value.
4. **What would reopen it:** the moving capture with GPS showing a correlated part much larger than at rest; #183's fixes changing the white σ, the 8 s arcs or the gap handling; a capture with longer unbroken arcs showing a significant inter-eNB drift.
5. **Sim validity row "LTE pseudorange"** (`verification.md`), proposed text: *Current source* "white σ against SNR and the at-rest correlated part: measured (20261110 static sessions, `analysis/results/lte-measured-noise`); multipath size, motion, told-vs-true position, dropouts in motion: TBC"; *Status* `measured` for white σ only, `placeholder` for the rest. **Shared tower clocks row:** *Real check* "sectors of one eNB drift together within 0.5 m/s (2 SE, `20261110-1759-static-out-long-e5060`, 1,800 s); between eNBs `TBD` (up to 24 m/s seen; 10.3 m/s with both cells above −8 dB), no bound". Not edited until the owners accept.

## Amendment 2026-11-11 (owners): the front end's real-capture fixes (#183, #245)

The owners accepted these front-end approaches on 2026-11-11, with the defaults #245 ships. Values stay `TBC`; the approaches are decided. Evidence: `analysis/results/lte-183-fixes/` (14 real captures, before and after #183's three fixes) and `analysis/results/lte-reacq-steps/` (the real static sessions `20261111-0151` and `-0233` against GPS truth, by double differences of two cells).

1. **The loop starts from the code rate, not the search's carrier offset** (`search_cfo_sigma_hz`, 1,000 Hz, `TBC`): the search's offset comes from the SSS-to-PSS phase, which another cell on the same PSS root corrupts (up to 1,700 Hz off on `20261110-lte-outside-5145` while its own 1σ said 20 to 360 Hz). Carrier aiding then starts on the right 200 Hz alias. On the 14 captures, cell tracks whose carrier offset is more than 0.1 ppm off their code rate went from 8 to 0.
2. **The shared PSS is weighted by its match to the SSS** (`pss_weight_alpha`, 0.05, `TBC`): in a frame-synchronised network the PSS is shared by every cell with that N_ID2; only the SSS is the cell's own. A cell alone on its root keeps both signals; one whose PSS is mostly another cell's is timed mostly on its SSS.
3. **An earlier path must be a path of this cell that the loop can track:** the PSS-to-SSS power ratio check of #245 (`first_path_ratio_db`, 3 dB, `TBC`), and, since the re-acquisition fix, the earlier peak must clear `first_path_floor` (10, `TBC`) times the delay profile's interference floor and be the largest value inside the code gate around it. Without the floor, a faded cell walked 1.6 to 7.6 samples earlier every 200 ms onto its neighbours' sync cross-correlation, a new arc each time. Those moves were 87 to 93 % of all new arcs on the two sessions; they were the "~700 m re-acquisition steps" and the "false locks" of PCI 244 and 220 in #214. After the fix: re-find steps over 100 m 96 to 99 % → 16 to 17 % (median 730 m → 10 m), and epochs with two cells ≥ −8 dB whose double difference is within 100 m of truth 9 to 11 % → 80 to 88 %.
4. **A re-find is not a dropped-sample gap when the host buffer stamps cover the stretch and show no stall:** the dongle loses whole USB transfers, which the stamps see. On `20261111-0233` one 12-sample re-find had been sized as a 19,188-sample gap, which moved every other cell by 1.9 km for the rest of the hour.
5. **Doppler records are off by default** (`Config.doppler = False`) until the carrier alias is resolved and checked on a real capture. This replaces the flag-and-drop of consequence 2: the 100 Hz offset it describes can sit on every cell, not only against the strongest one. The carrier phase is known only modulo the 200 Hz half-frame rate, and modulo 100 Hz if subframes 0 and 5 reach the dongle with different phases (TS 36.211 leaves the sync signals' antenna port to the eNB); nothing checks a record against its code rate. On `20261111-0151` and `-0233`, 49 to 52 % of two-cell doppler differences (zero at rest) sat on a non-zero 100 Hz alias (34.3 m/s) while each record was told about 0.1 m/s. The sim's front-end-shaped records carry pseudoranges only already.
