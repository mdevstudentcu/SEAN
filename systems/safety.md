**Regulation, as it applies (our reading, not legal advice).** Three regimes touch this prototype: spectrum (ISED: the payload is receive-only and our only emitters are licence-exempt links, *RF and spectrum*); interception (Radiocommunication Act s.9(2): we decode only what is meant for everyone, *Legal*); and aviation (we fly only indoors in a netted area; outdoor flight near YOW needs the Transport Canada certificate and NAV CANADA authorization named in *Flight*, ASM-06). Privacy is handled by design at capture (*Privacy*). Mentor confirmation is `TBD`, and the product needs a legal opinion (`roadmap.md`, *Certification*).

## RF and spectrum

- **Receive-only payload** (REQ-07, ADR-0001). The only emitters on the vehicle are licence-exempt: the 2.4 GHz RC link (FlySky FS-i6), Wi-Fi to the ground laptop, and (if used) 915 MHz telemetry. The ESP32 RC bridge's 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi access point (ADR-0029) is retired with the bridge. The flight controller's MAVLink is to reach the Pi on its TX line and ground only (planned wiring), so the Pi cannot send to it (ADR-0028, *Amendment 2026-11-11*).
- **No jamming or spoofing, ever.** These are simulated in software (ASM-03). GNSS jammers are prohibited in Canada, and the organizers warned that anything drawing an ISED Spectrum Management call nearly rules a team out.
- No SDR with transmit capability is used (HackRF excluded for this reason).

## Legal: interception of radiocommunication

- Radiocommunication Act s.9(2) prohibits intercepting **and using** a radiocommunication without permission from its originator or intended recipient. We decode only signals intended for general reception: synchronization and reference signals, pilots, beacons, navaid signals, broadcasts, and ADS-B. **No user or communication content is decoded or stored** (REQ-08).
- This is our reading, not legal advice; confirmed with mentors: `TBD`.

## Privacy

- Wi-Fi BSSIDs are salted and hashed on the board that scans, before they leave it (ADR-0011, ADR-0018). Client probe requests are never captured.
- ADS-B data is public broadcast; we store it but publish only aggregates.
- Camera footage stays local and is not published without review.

## Flight

- Independent RC link with a physical kill switch is always present; software never removes the pilot's ability to take over (ADR-0028, which supersedes ADR-0012). Without it the drone doesn't fly. The link is a FlySky FS-i6 transmitter and FS-iA6B receiver; **SwB is the kill switch** (ARM off disarms) (ADR-0028, *Amendment 2026-11-11 (owners)*).
- **RC loss disarms; it does not land.** The receiver's failsafe sends throttle low and ARM off, so Betaflight disarms. If the receiver's frames stop altogether, Betaflight's own failsafe (`failsafe_procedure` `DROP`) stops the motors. Either way the drone falls from where it is: there is no controlled landing. Checked on the bench 2026-11-11: with SwB armed and the transmitter off, channel 5 dropped to 1000. The receiver's default is to hold the last values, which keeps the drone armed, so the failsafe is set in the transmitter and tested before flying (`flight/README.md`).
- The ESP32 Wi-Fi control link for tethered hover (ADR-0029) is retired: the RC link replaces it, and the flashed Betaflight build has no CRSF driver to read the bridge. ADR-0029's tether limits, for any tethered hover (`TBC`, owners):
  - A floor-anchored tether of about 0.5 m, hovering at 0.3–0.5 m, with prop guards, padding and an XT60 loop plug as the kill on the ground.
- Flights indoors in a netted area, with prop guards, a speed limit and the RC-loss failsafe above. No geofence: this Betaflight setup has none.
- Carleton appears to be inside YOW controlled airspace: no outdoor flights without the required Transport Canada certificate and NAV CANADA authorization (ASM-06).
- LiPo handling: charge in a LiPo bag, never unattended; damaged packs are retired.

## Cybersecurity and integrity of the navigation solution

- Spoofing is treated as an adversary, not noise: the estimator cross-checks GPS against independent sources (REQ-04).
- Sources that fail together with GPS (ADS-B content, GNSS-disciplined tower timing) are not counted as independent evidence.
- Live stream is on a local network only; no remote control path exists through the payload stream. It is authenticated and encrypted with CurveZMQ, and plaintext needs an explicit `--insecure` (ADR-0026, #89).
