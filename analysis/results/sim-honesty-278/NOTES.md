# Reading the #278 sensitivity study

Evidence: **sim**, placeholder environments (ADR-0015), commit `20aa471`, seeds 0–29 paired (30 runs per row; #278 asks for at least 20). `summary.md` and `sensitivity.png` are generated by `analysis/sim_honesty.py`; this file is the reading. Every term is off in every committed scenario; the study switches each on alone, then together.

## Result

- **No term moves T1 in a way these legs can show.** Both legs are far from the 25 m target before any term is added: `challenge-drone-outage` T1 13.3 km (`ekf`) and 9.6 km (`ekf-calibrated-measured`), each run's median p95 4.5 and 3.5 km; `realistic-long-outage` 701 m and 1,444 m. Terms of metres to tens of metres sit under that. On the drone leg a small change flips which LTE records a run accepts, so T1 swings by ±1 to 5 km in both directions while the paired median change per seed stays near 0 m (tropo, sector bias, baro, holdover, steering). Those T1 swings are not read as effects.
- **Exactly zero, as the model says:** the gyro term on both legs (neither estimator reads the IMU), and the sector bias on `realistic` (one tower per site there, so each tower's own clock state absorbs a constant). Network-held holdover (30 ns in 3 h) is under 1 m.
- **Arc restarts matter, for the filter configured as on real data.** `ekf` with `arc_bias` on (what it does by itself on a real capture, ADR-0050): each run's p95 grows by a median 605 m (20 of 30 seeds worse), and the share of epochs above HPL goes from 0.35 to 0.59. With `all` terms: +713 m, 18 of 30, 0.58. Plain `ekf` on the sim's records (arc bias off): median 0 m. So the arc bias state, not the arcs alone, is what costs here; why was not examined.
- **The near-far floor is the other term that changes which records are used.** At −6 dB (fewer cells heard) `ekf`'s per-run p95 falls by a median 889 m (16 of 30 better): fewer towers is better with the leg's committed LTE error, as #208 found. `ekf-calibrated-measured` gets worse (+122 m median). At −20 dB `ekf`'s median is unchanged (14 of 30 worse) and `ekf-calibrated-measured` gets worse (+367 m).
- **Steering jumps on `realistic`:** per-run p95 falls by a median 10 m (`ekf`) and 24 m (`ekf-calibrated-measured`, 21 of 30 seeds better), while `ekf-calibrated-measured`'s T1 rises 137 m. Not examined.
- **Above HPL:** only the arc-bias rows move it by more than 0.05 (drone, 0.35 → 0.59). `floor6` lowers it (0.35 → 0.26, `ekf`).

## Recommendation

None of the terms is recommended as a default in the challenge set now, so no ADR was written. Each would change every published number (a sim change breaks pairing, `systems/quality.md`) and none shows a measurable effect on T1 at today's error levels. The arc restarts are the candidate once a leg reaches tens of metres (#262's measured ladder reaches 105 m): they are measured on our own captures and are the one term that moves integrity here.
