# Sim honesty: sensitivity to the missing error terms (#278)

Evidence: **sim**, placeholder environments (ADR-0015). Commit `b9f414a`; seeds 0-29 (30 runs per row), paired: every term draws on a stream of its own. Generated by `analysis/sim_honesty.py`.

T1: worst second of the per-second 95th percentile of horizontal error across runs, 0-300 s after GPS loss (ADR-0034). Above HPL: share of epochs in that window with error over HPL, mean run (worst run). Δ: against `base`, same estimator and seeds. Paired: each run's own 95th percentile of error over the window, minus the same seed's in `base`; the median over seeds, and how many seeds got worse by more than 1 m.
