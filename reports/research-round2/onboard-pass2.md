## A. Map matching — findings so far
- Kinnari, Verdoja, Kyrki RA-L 2022 (arXiv 2110.01967) real flights [V]: 3 flights at 91-92 m, 4.1-6.9 km, Sweden, summer map vs Oct/Nov flights (leaf-off). Mean error after convergence 26.5 / 29.1 / 30.6 m; convergence after about 2 km. Prior: 1000 particles in 100x100 m around true start, no yaw prior. Forest least reliable (tall-tree distortion); lake: flat score, filter leans on odometry. Truth RTK. CAVEAT: images orthoprojected with ground-truth pose and DEM. i7 + Quadro RTX 3000: 0.33 s patch extraction + 0.13 s inference, update every 100 m.
- NaviLoc (Shpagin, Zenodo 17779254, Dec 2025, CC BY 4.0) [V]: 20.38 m ATE; 31x better than VIO drift; MobileNet-V3; VIO-heading rectification + sliding-window SE(2). Published as Drones 10(2) 97, 2026 [S]; 19.5 m MLE, 9 FPS Pi 5 [S].
- OrthoTrack (Dhaouadi ... Pollefeys, Cremers; arXiv 2606.25245, ECCV 2026) [V abstract]: training-free; orthophoto + surface model keyframe anchoring + optical-flow propagation; metric 6-DoF every frame; MovingDrone dataset (photorealistic). Per-frame failure rate <0.3 % on three benchmarks [S].
- UAV Navigation-Grupo Oesia VNS01 press release 2026-09-02 [S]: TRN + satellite map matching, "approximately 30 m" over unflown routes.
- Bearing-UAV (CVPR 2026, arXiv 2603.22153) [V abstract]: joint location+heading, Bearing-UAV-90k benchmark; no numbers.
- Choate and Rife (Tufts), ION GNSS+ 2025, arXiv 2602.14311 [V abstract]: aircraft taxi SfM mosaic to satellite; SfM drift limits gain; integrity features flag registration anomalies and ambiguous matches.
- Zhu, Meurer, Guenther, "Integrity of Visual Navigation", NAVIGATION 69(2) navi.518, 2022 [S]: integrity framework; over-conservative PL hurts continuity.
- Xiao, Tortei, Roura, Loianno, "Long-range UAV Thermal Geo-localization with Satellite Imagery", IROS 2023, arXiv 2306.02994 [V abstract]: predecessor of STHN; Boson-nighttime dataset released; no numbers in abstract.
- UAV-VisLoc dataset (Zenodo 19603534) [S]: 14,998 images, 30 trajectories (21 real), RGB and IR, 160-2,500 m.
- ISPRS 2026 (Wang, Tsou, Jhan, Tang; NTUST/NCSIST) [V landing]: 50 cm orthophoto, 1 m DSM, 25 km2, Sept 2025 flights; "meter-level"; per-altitude table only in PDF (not readable with WebFetch).
- Remote Sensing 18(5) 752, 2026, doi 10.3390/rs18050752, "A Multimodal Feature Fusion Framework for UAV Positioning in Weak GNSS Environments Using a Priori High-Resolution Satellite Imagery" [S abstract]: mean real-time error 0.73 m at 80 m altitude rising to 6.24 m at 480 m. Uses winter and summer drone images against summer satellite; LoFTR 4.18 m, RoMa 2.48 m on their data [S]. Ground object type, season, altitude and satellite scale "significantly influence" accuracy.
- Sensors 24(17) 5491, 2024 [S]: 80-200 m, 81.7 % within 20 m; altitude estimate error 18.1 %; models trained at 80-100 m degrade at other altitudes.
- Sci. Reports 2025 s41598-025-01468-3 (HTransformer) [S]: snow-covered satellite scenes lose texture so summer reference used; winter drone data matched worse than summer.
- Sensors 26(13) 4165, 2026 [S]: satellite-to-aerial registration across spectral bands; about 2.8 m independent accuracy for four matchers incl. LoFTR and RoMa.
- Sensors 26(4) 1322, 2026 [S]: RoMa best up to 45 deg rotation; MatchAnything variant best beyond 60 deg (yaw prior matters).
- Embedded compute: LightGlue on RK3588 CPU about 840 ms per pair; FastGlue 666 ms (ISPRS Annals XI-2-2026-45) [S]. On a desktop i7 CPU: LightGlue pipeline about 1058 ms vs XFeat-only about 126 ms (arXiv 2607.04811) [S]. Still no Pi 5 SuperPoint/LightGlue figure.
- Cross day-night geo-registration preprint (preprints.org 202502.0617) [S]: over 344.6 km flown, RMSE under 50 m; visible-image methods "usually cannot work well" at night.
- PiLoT v2 (arXiv 2606.31098, June 2026) [V abstract]: replaces PiLoT's 3D mesh with orthophoto+DSM crops, CPU-friendly; adds gravity and single-point laser range priors (OPTICAL if laser used). No numbers in abstract.
- OrthoLoC (Dhaouadi, Marin, Meier, Kaiser, Cremers; NeurIPS 2025, arXiv 2509.18350) [V abstract]: 16,425 UAV images (Germany, US) against orthographic geodata; AdHoP refinement: matching up to 95 % better, translation error up to 63 % lower; studies resolution effects (numbers in full text only).
- SWA-PF (arXiv 2509.13795, 2025) [V abstract]: semantic-weighted particle filter, 4-DoF, low-res satellite maps, "below 10 m", seconds per pose; MAFS multi-altitude dataset.
- GISNav (github hmakelin/gisnav) [V README]: MIT licence, ROS 2, PX4 SITL only; README says not for real flights; no accuracy.
- ArduPilot GSoC 2024 "High Altitude Non-GPS Navigation" (forum) [V]: SIFT to a stitched map + Lucas-Kanade flow, fed via MAV_CMD_EXTERNAL_POSITION_ESTIMATE; needs >= 4 Hz matching; ArduPilot wind dead reckoning holds about 30 s; flow data at 75 m; no error figures; code ap_nongps, licence unstated. This is the precursor of NGPS.
- WildNav (github TIERS/wildnav, arXiv 2210.09727) [S]: the 15.8 m mean in round 1 section 7.3 is this; licence not confirmed.
- Jurevicius et al. (Vilnius), arXiv 1910.12121, PF + VO [S]: similar accuracy with a map dated 2014, six years older than the flight imagery. Only map-age number found.
- FoundLoc (He, Cisneros, Keetha, Patrikar ... Scherer, CMU; arXiv 2310.16299) [V abstract]: nadir camera + IMU + satellite imagery, VIO + foundation-model VPR, no initial pose needed; average "within 20 m", minimum below 1 m; real-world appearance change; resource-constrained platforms.
- Bianchi and Barfoot (U. Toronto), "UAV Localization Using Autoencoded Satellite Images", RA-L 2021, arXiv 2102.05692 [V abstract]: Google Earth images along the planned path compressed by an autoencoder; six real-UAV runs over one day's lighting; RMSE under 3 m at about 1 % of the runtime of the standard approach. Needs a path prior.
- Citation chase (OpenAlex, papers citing Kinnari RA-L 2022 or LSVL, 2024-2026: 55 titles) [V list]. Not opened but relevant for the next pass: "The Kidnapped UAV Problem: Self-Supervised Detection and Recovery of Localization Failures for GNSS-Denied Navigation" (ION 2026; not found by search); "Real-Time UAV Navigation Using Machine Learning, Satellite Images, and Continuously Operating High-Speed Cameras" (NAVIGATION 2026; not found by search); "Nighttime UAV Absolute Visual Localization via Dual-Exposure Feature Fusion" (IEEE TGRS 2026); "In-Flight Initialization of Global Visual-Inertial Estimators using Geospatial Data" (IROS 2024); "Multi Map Visual Localization for UAVs" (RA-L 2024); "A robust visual localization framework for UAVs using a zero-shot aerial-satellite image matcher and a particle filter" (Satellite Navigation 2026); "A Novel Scene-Matching Positioning Approach for Low-Altitude UAVs Considering Elevation Differences in Satellite Images" (IEEE IoT J 2026); VecMapLocNet (vector maps, ISPRS J 2025); "Absolute Drone Localization Using Camera Imagery and Map Data" (Brno thesis 2026). Survey: "GNSS-denied UAV navigation: analyzing computational complexity, sensor fusion, and localization methodologies", Satellite Navigation 2025 (160 citations).
- SatLoc dataset and fusion (Remote Sensing 17(17) 3048, 2025) [S]: micro-UAV, satellite matching + inter-frame matching + optical flow with altitude; absolute error below 15 m at over 2 Hz on a 6 TOPS edge computer; 100-300 m rotorcraft flights.
- SLUG (Sensors 26(19) 6030, 2026) [S]: cross-view urban self-localisation; pure visual R@1 only 88.24 % at the 100 m threshold, ambiguity in symmetric scenes.
- Smaller items [S]: SatLoc benchmark (Remote Sensing 17(17) 3048; 100-300 m rotorcraft); CS-UAV set, 9 trajectories of about 10 km (arXiv 2506.09748); day-and-night drone-view geo-localization benchmark (arXiv 2607.25778) says current methods work mainly by day; NIVnet near-IR to visible registration (IEEE TVT 2024); Lee, Mesic, Chung (Caltech) self-supervised landmarks for terrain-relative navigation across seasons; DiffusionUavLoc (arXiv 2511.06422) addresses snow but only on University-1652 (partly synthetic); USENIX VehicleSec 2025 WIP: false localization attacks on vSLAM drones gave up to 252 m error and 70 m altitude deviation in simulation.

## B. Venue sweep and fielded systems — findings
- ION GNSS+ 2025, Silva, Hernandez, de Souza Jr, "Airspeed-Aided Inertial Navigation with Pseudovanes and Non-Optical Sensors for GNSS- and Vision-Denied UAV Environments", doi 10.33012/2025.20274 [V abstract]: real VTOL flight over desert; pitot, magnetometer, barometer, temperature; pseudovane zero-velocity updates on lateral/vertical airspeed; 14 architectures compared; best (tightly coupled, navigation-frame wind states) gives mean horizontal drift "0.86 m/s" (a rate; check full text). ## C1. Optical flow at altitude without a rangefinder — findings
- Nguyen, Rosser, Perera, Moss, Teague, Chahl, "Characteristics of optical flow from aerial thermal imaging, 'thermal flow'", J. Field Robotics 39(5) 580-599, 2022, doi 10.1002/rob.22065 (CC BY-NC) [S abstract]: LWIR flow on an aircraft over a 24 h window; agrees with a visible flow sensor by day; reliable from midday to late evening, then degrades until shortly after sunrise (thermal crossover). A follow-up from the group flew closed- and open-loop on a small UAV in visual darkness with LWIR flow "equivalent" to the benchmark flow sensor [S]. Sim implication: thermal flow availability is time-of-day dependent, worst pre-dawn.
- Cellini, Boyacioglu, Lopez, van Breugel, "Discovering and exploiting active sensing motifs for estimation", arXiv 2511.08766 (2025-2026) [V abstract]: on real GPS-denied quadcopter data, altitude, ground speed and wind direction from optic flow plus acceleration become observable only during active manoeuvres (accelerations/decelerations); estimator accurate only in high-observability periods. Python package pybounds. No numbers in abstract.
- Optical Engineering 58(8) 083102, 2019 [S]: flow-aided INS on fixed-wing; height observable under a flat-terrain assumption (simulation and experiment).
- Event-camera flow on a Raspberry Pi 5 for altitude estimation and landing, effective at 30 lux where a frame camera failed [S; paper and year not confirmed, likely arXiv 2507.15444 or 2509.08235].
- Chmielewski and Sibilski, "Ground Speed Optical Estimator for Miniature UAV", Sensors 21(8) 2754, 2021 [S]: downward camera on a miniature fixed-wing, SIL then hardware flights; error figures not retrieved (MDPI blocked).
- arXiv 1901.07278 [S]: Pi-class ego-motion sensor vs a PX4Flow on a hexacopter in a park: 0.076 m/s mean difference (sensor vs sensor, not truth).
- ArduPilot forum (2025) [S]: flow without a rangefinder works but drifts; baro height "bleeds" into the flow scale; consistent with round 2's scale-error model. Nothing quantitative.
- Still not found: any outdoor flow-velocity error curve against height with GNSS/RTK truth.

## C2. VIO/VO drift at 30-150 m, without alignment, on embedded hardware — findings
- Inertial Labs VINS flight campaign (web page "VINS GNSS-Denied Navigation Performance Across Multiple Flight Tests", 2025 flights, vendor) [V table]: Cessna 172, 18 flights Apr-Jul 2025, average AGL about 190-1,020 m, GNSS denied 15-46 min and 40-153 km per flight; per-flight position error 7.2-35.1 m (overall mean 16.55 m); IR camera mean 15.88 m vs daylight 18.08 m; against "onboard 3D satellite maps". Error over distance 0.010-0.056 % [C from table]. Page claims "below 0.2 % for most flights" and "never exceeded 0.5 % over 100 km". This is map-anchored (belongs with A too), not free VIO. Table has a typo (one Avg AGL above Max AGL).
- Sa et al., "Build Your Own Visual-Inertial Drone", arXiv 1708.06652 (2017) [S]: about 180 m farm flight, 0.82 % of distance, low-cost onboard computer. Short and low; supports the 1-3 % monocular model only loosely.
- Ibrahimov, Yang, Mueller (Berkeley HiPeRLab), "Kalman Filter-Based Drift Detection and Mitigation of VIO in UAVs", ACC 2025 pp. 457-462, doi 10.23919/ACC63710.2025.11107817 [S]: KF-based detector for VIO drift, validated offline on real flights; no numbers retrieved. Method for an integrity monitor on VIO.
- Delaune-style night thermal-inertial work and NASA's "Thermal-Inertial UAV Night Dataset" (data.nasa.gov) [S]. An April 2026 preprint (arXiv 2604.26201) states thermal-inertial odometry still "accumulates drift over long trajectories" outdoors. No new TIO drift percentage beyond the round-2 "under 2 %".
- Jeon, Jung, Myung, "Run Your VIO on NVIDIA Jetson", ICRA 2021, arXiv 2103.01655 [S]: VINS-Mono/Fusion, Kimera, ROVIO, ORB-SLAM2 etc. on TX2, Xavier NX, AGX; indoor dataset; accuracy, CPU and memory. No Pi or Orin Nano; nothing outdoor.
- Not found: per-minute yaw drift for outdoor VIO; a run-level VIO failure rate on real outdoor flights.

## C3. Learned inertial odometry outdoors, over minutes, rotor-speed methods — findings
- OPsCV (JATM 2026, see B) [S]: learned deep inertial odometry + cross-view geolocalisation, evaluated on post-processed IMU data from a real UAV mission with the Brazilian Air Force. The only outdoor real-flight learned-inertial item found; numbers not readable.
- AT-LSTM, IEEE Sensors J. 2024 (doi via IEEE 10521462) [S]: LSTM+attention compensates INS error during GNSS outages; "more than 90 %" better than INS within 60 s; simulation and "actual experiments"; platform unclear. Relative figure only.
- GRU-aided adaptive KF, Remote Sensing 14 752 (2022) [S]: horizontal error 16.12 % and 31.71 % lower than GRU-aided KF after 120 s and 180 s outages; not UAV-specific.
- Datasets with rotor speed or RPM and outdoor truth (owner go-ahead needed before download) [S]: INSANE (AAU; outdoor, dual RTK truth, vibration data with RPM); KIOS drone dataset on Zenodo (v2 May 2026; DJI M300 RTK, ESC speed, outdoor); MUN-FRL (Memorial Univ. of Newfoundland, 2024; visual-inertial-lidar, RTK truth; no rotor speed); VID dataset (2021; rotor speed, motor current). AI-IO (arXiv 2603.00597) dataset: real quadrotor with rotor speed; truth type not confirmed.
- Rotor-speed and drag background [S]: ENAC, IMAV 2022, drag coefficient estimated from outdoor flight by least squares; Allibert et al. body-frame velocity from accelerometer + rotor speed/torque; ANU (Bangura 2017) ESC-computed thrust from electrical power and rotor speed. No outdoor velocity RMSE in m/s found.
- The main gap from round 2 stands: no learned inertial odometry result that is outdoors, minutes long, with GNSS truth, in wind, on an unseen vehicle.

## C4. Dynamics, drag and airspeed aiding — findings
- ION GNSS+ 2025 pseudovane/pitot VTOL flight (see B) [V abstract]: best mean horizontal drift "0.86 m/s"; pitot without full wind states is worse than without pitot. Sim implication: airspeed aiding needs explicit wind states, and the residual behaves as a velocity bias of order 1 m/s.
- Sharma, Laupre, Skaloud NAVIGATION 2023 (see B) [V]: VDM beats MEMS INS over long outages but not a 0.3 deg/h IMU over 2 min; ADIS-class INS reached 1 km at 2 min.
- Khaghani and Skaloud, "Assessment of VDM-based autonomous navigation of a UAV under operational conditions", Robotics and Autonomous Systems 106 152-164, 2018, doi 10.1016/j.robot.2018.05.007 [S abstract]: real custom fixed-wing flight + Monte Carlo; 1-2 orders of magnitude better than INS during 3 min outages; filter uncertainty consistent; compute at most about 3x INS (thesis). Upgrades round 2's "Khaghani RAS 2018 not reached" from nothing to [S].
- IEEE Access, Jan 2025 [S]: tightly coupled VDM on a small fixed-wing, real flight, about one order of magnitude better than tightly coupled INS/GNSS with two satellites over a 100 s outage (may be a re-listing of the 2020 Access paper; not confirmed).
- Gilgien and Skaloud, AHORN 2025 talk [S]: multi-IMU VDM on fixed-wing with simulated outage; adaptation to delta-wing.
- Multirotor VDM [S]: Ko et al., IEEE Access 2019 (EKF/UKF, propeller-speed-to-thrust model from flight); Mueller et al. 2016 quadrotor result cited as "significant improvement" during outages. No metres.
- Hot-wire MEMS flow sensor in a "wind barrel" on a multirotor [S, paper not identified]: wind-speed error about 0.22 m/s in outdoor hover and below 0.38 m/s in dynamic flight. This is the first outdoor multirotor air-relative velocity accuracy found; consistent with round 2's 0.2-0.6 m/s wind-at-outage-start prior.
- PX4 drag fusion (still nothing): no published velocity accuracy; the PR's flight results exist only as PDF plots; GNSS dead-reckoning mode documented as limited real-world testing [S].

## C5. Lidar and radar odometry and altimeters on small drones (all TX or OPTICAL) — findings
- Zhu et al., "Robust 4D Radar-aided Inertial Navigation for Aerial Vehicles", arXiv 2502.15452, 2025 [V full-text tables]: Meituan delivery drone, down-facing Continental ARS548 4D radar (77 GHz, 20 Hz, 200-300 points/frame), ADIS16470 IMU, RTK-INS truth, 30-100 m altitude, flights of 670-1,010 (units unstated, likely m). APE RMSE without prior map: 0.59-3.68 m (houses and greenhouse 0.6-1.6 m; grass 2.3-3.7 m); Doppler-only EKF-RIO 2.9-8.4 m; FAST-LIO (lidar) diverged on 6 of 10 flights including all grass flights and gave 2.8-18.4 m where it ran. With a prior map: 0.35-2.6 m. Rotation RMSE 0.7-1.7 deg. APE is usually computed after trajectory alignment, so treat as optimistic [C]. TX. Sim implications: radar RIO error roughly 0.1-0.4 % of distance [C], worst over grass (featureless); lidar odometry fails over flat grass at these heights.
- arXiv 2603.14109 (2026) [S]: hierarchical radar-inertial odometry with two coupled factor graphs, 27x real time; no drift figures in snippet. TX.
- Michalczyk et al., ICRA 2023 multi-state EKF RIO [S]: global pose drift reduced by a factor of 4 on average vs prior RIO. TX.
- "Parameter Sensitivity Analysis for Aerial LiDAR-Inertial Odometries in low-altitude flights", arXiv 2609.12837 (2026) [S]: FAST-LIO2 and Cartographer on two public quadrotor datasets, three lidars; high altitude left to future work. OPTICAL.
- Altimeters [S]: Ainstein US-D1 110 g, 108 x 79 x 20 mm, 0.3-50 m, 100 Hz; price cut after 20,000 units (price not given); US-D1 Pro 0.5 to 120+ m, CAN FD (May 2025); Ainstein LR-D1 (longer range, for MALE drones and helicopters); Saglamtek BORADAR-50/100/200 (24 GHz FMCW); Meteksan CRA C-band to 5,000 ft. All TX.
- TU Delft/TNO 2021 "Radar-Aided Navigation System for Small Drones in GPS-Denied Environments" [S]: omnidirectional radar estimates velocity, course and height; simulation + experiment. TX.
- Still not found: a commercial drone Doppler-velocity radar (other than Honeywell's) with a percent-of-distance spec.
